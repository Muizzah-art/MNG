## Test Meeting Script — 5 Speakers 

**Scenario:** Weekly product team standup for a fictional app called "Loop."
**Speakers:** **Amina** (Project Manager), **Yusuf** (Backend Engineer), **Khadijah** (Designer), **Maryam** (QA), **Ibrahim** (Marketing)

---

**Amina:** Okay, let's get started. Thanks, everyone, for joining. Today's agenda is the sprint review, the login bug, and next month's marketing push. Yusuf, can you give us the backend update?

**Yusuf:** Sure. The API refactor is finished. The main thing left is the caching layer, which is improving response times by around 40%. I just need to finish the tests.

**Amina:** Any blockers?

**Yusuf:** No, just about two more days of work.

**Khadijah:** I also finished the new onboarding mockups. I reduced the flow from five screens to three and moved the permissions request later so it feels less overwhelming.

**Maryam:** I reviewed them. They look great, but the skip and continue buttons on the second screen are too close together. I nearly tapped the wrong one.

**Khadijah:** Good catch. I'll fix the spacing today.

**Amina:** Perfect. Let's make that change before development starts.

**Maryam:** One more thing—the login bug is still happening. Some users are getting logged out after about ten minutes.

**Yusuf:** I think it's a token refresh issue. I can prioritize it before the caching work.

**Amina:** Great. Let's do that.

**Ibrahim:** From marketing's side, I'd rather delay the announcement until the login issue is fixed. We don't want to attract new users while that's still happening.

**Amina:** Agreed. Ibrahim, please move it to next week.

**Ibrahim:** Will do. I'll also prepare a short list of the key improvements for the launch post.

**Khadijah:** I'll send you screenshots of the new onboarding flow.

**Ibrahim:** Thanks, that'll help.

**Amina:** Perfect. So, Yusuf tackles the login bug first, Khadijah updates the design, Maryam retests the fix, and Ibrahim prepares the launch materials. Anything else?

**Yusuf:** Nothing from me.

**Khadijah:** All good.

**Maryam:** Nothing else.

**Ibrahim:** All set.

**Amina:** Great. Thanks, everyone. See you next week.
